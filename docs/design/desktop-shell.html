Behavior contract

The rules an implementation must reproduce.
  • Focus: click raises and focuses; unfocused chrome mutes title and tile. The ring shows only for keyboard focus (:focus-visible).
  • Snap: dragging to an edge previews a frosted zone; dropping stores the pre-drag rect; repeat-snap or maximize restores it exactly.
  • Workspaces: a window belongs to one workspace; dock clicks follow it across; the switcher shows the current workspace only.
  • Sessions: apps are session-local; closing discards the document and announces it.
  • Notifications: toasts cap at 3, auto-dismiss in 6 s, DND mutes; the bell counts unread until the center opens.
  • Apps: every app ships navigation, loading, empty, and failure states — loading uses skeletons, failure always offers recovery.
  • Motion: reduced-motion replaces fades with cuts and spinners with written state.
  • Palette: typing anywhere opens search; Esc unwinds one layer at a time.
