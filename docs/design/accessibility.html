02 — Visible focus

Focus rings

One ring everywhere: 2 px accent with a 2 px offset, meeting 3:1 against every surface. Rings appear for keyboard focus (:focus-visible) and stay quiet for pointer clicks — clicking is already visible feedback.

04 — Display scaling

Scaling

The layout is vector- and token-driven, so scaling is a multiplier, not a redesign. Text reflows, targets grow, borders stay crisp. Drag from 100% to 200% and watch the window and dock reflow.
